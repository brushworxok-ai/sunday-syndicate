Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

Information we collect

The app stores information needed to operate a private league, including player name, phone number, sign-in credentials in protected form, picks, preferences, payment handles or payment-status claims, chat messages, notification settings, and consent history.

How we use information

We use this information to run league features, authenticate players, calculate standings, provide account support, deliver requested notifications, prevent abuse, and maintain an audit trail.

Text messaging data

If you separately opt in, we may send verification codes, pick reminders, results, and league announcements. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

We do not sell or share text-messaging originator opt-in data or consent with third parties for their promotional or marketing purposes. We may provide the minimum information necessary to service providers that help operate the program, such as hosting, communications, database, and AI-processing vendors.

Choices and retention

You may turn off SMS in My Profile or reply STOP. You may disable push notifications in My Profile or in your device settings. League records are retained while needed to operate, secure, and document the league. Ask the commissioner to correct or remove account information when appropriate.

Security and children

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. This app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Contact the league commissioner through the app’s Chat or My Profile area. For SMS assistance, reply HELP to the sending number.
